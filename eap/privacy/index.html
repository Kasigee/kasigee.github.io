EAP privacy

What EAP keeps, what it doesn't, and how to have it deleted.

Environment Adjusted Pace (EAP) is a small service run by Kasimir Gregory in Armidale, NSW. This page says what EAP collects, why, where it's kept, and how to have it deleted. Questions or requests go to kasimir [at] live.com.au.

What EAP collects

  • Waitlist. Your email address, and if you give them, your first name and watch brand, with the date you joined and the page you joined from.
  • Strava connection. Your Strava athlete ID and first name, the sign-in tokens Strava issues to EAP, and the settings you choose: weight, height, whether you live at altitude, whether to show laps, and whether EAP is paused.
  • Your runs. When you upload a run to Strava, EAP reads it (time, distance, GPS track, elevation, speed and laps), works out EAP, and adds a short block to the run's description. The run itself isn't stored: it's held in memory while EAP is worked out, then dropped.
  • FIT uploads on this website. A file you upload to "Try it on a run" is read once to work out EAP and isn't kept.
  • Server logs. The service keeps short technical logs (time, page requested, errors, and your IP address for rate limiting). They can include a run's EAP summary line, but not the run itself.
  • This website. Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

Weight and height are used only to work out EAP for your runs. EAP doesn't collect heart rate.

Who else sees it

  • Weather. To get the weather for a run, EAP sends a single point near the middle of the run (to about 10 m) and the run's start and end times to Open-Meteo, or to Visual Crossing if Open-Meteo is down. No name or account details are sent with it.
  • Hosting. The service and its database run on Google Cloud in Sydney (Cloud Run and Firestore). Emails are sent through Gmail.
  • Strava. The EAP block is written to your own run's description, so it's seen by whoever can see that run on Strava, under your Strava privacy settings.

EAP doesn't sell your data, share it with other users, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

How long it's kept

  • Strava sign-in and settings: until you disconnect. Disconnecting from EAP's settings page deletes them straight away. Removing EAP on Strava (Settings → My Apps) tells EAP to delete them too.
  • Waitlist entries: until you ask to be removed, or until EAP closes.
  • Server logs: about 30 days.
  • EAP blocks already added to your runs stay on Strava until you edit or delete them, because they're part of your run's description.

Your choices

From EAP's settings page you can change your details, pause EAP, or disconnect and delete everything. To see what EAP holds about you, correct it, or have a waitlist entry removed, email kasimir [at] live.com.au and it will be done within 30 days, usually much sooner. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Estimates, not measurements

EAP's numbers are estimates from published research on hills, altitude, wind and heat. They aren't medical or coaching advice.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the change will be noted here and on EAP's settings page. The date at the top shows the last update.
