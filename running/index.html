Environment Adjusted Pace

A pace on a hilly, windy morning at altitude isn't the same effort as the same pace on a flat, still, sea-level day. Environment Adjusted Pace (EAP) estimates what each run would have been worth on that ideal day, point by point along the GPS track, and shows where the time went.

It combines published models for hills (grade-adjusted pace), heat and humidity (dew point), wind (speed and direction against the way you're running, corrected from mast height to runner height), altitude, surface, air quality and tight corners. Weather comes from Open-Meteo for the time and place of the run.

A Strava plugin that adds this line to every run is in testing on my own account. Once it opens up, you'll be able to connect here.
