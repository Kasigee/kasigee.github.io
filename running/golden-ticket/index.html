25-week training plan

13–14 March 2027 · Civic Park, Armidale

Golden Ticket race blueprint

75.5% of the cumulative scoreboard sits in Saturday's 10 km and Sunday morning's Half Marathon. Winning means controlled precision early and ruthless turnaround execution.

Window Event Clock Share Target Effort Tactical Rule

Saturday · 12:00 → 4:40 pm

Between-race turnaround

  • 0–5 min post-finish: 3–5 min easy flush jog/walk to clear blood lactate twice as fast as sitting down.
  • 5–15 min: Shade, legs elevated, ice/cold wet towel on neck & forearms to curb March afternoon heat drift.
  • Every major gap: 30–40 g fast carbs + 300–500 mg sodium (~100–130 g total across Saturday afternoon) before the 4 pm 10 km.

Overnight · 14-hour reload

Sat 4:40 pm → Sun 7:00 am

  • First 4 hours (peak glycogen window): ~300–350 g low-fibre carbs (80–90 g/h) + ~70 g protein + 1.5× sweat-loss electrolytes starting within 15 min of the 10 km.
  • Heat prep (Weeks 19–21): 3 × 25–30 min post-run sauna sessions per week to expand plasma volume ahead of Saturday afternoon warmth.
  • Sunday 7:00 am Half: Conservative km 1–5 while legs open up, 60–80 g/h carbs in-race, negative split over the final 6 km.
